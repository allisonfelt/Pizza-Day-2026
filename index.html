The car shuttle

The only genuinely tricky part of the day
Getting there
  1. Julia, Allison, Eric and Laura leave the city in Eric's car. Allison will start a group chat.
  2. Andreina, Corinne, Adam and Arden leave Oakland in Adam's car.
  3. Both cars meet at the sandwich shop. There's a Whole Foods and a Trader Joe's five minutes away — split up and do lunch and snacks at the same time.
At Steelhead
  1. Everyone except the drivers unloads at Steelhead with the float gear.
  2. Bec and Sienna arrive and park their car at one end.
  3. All non-float gear goes in Adam's secret compartment 😉
  4. Non-drivers stay at Steelhead and start pumping 💪
  5. Eric and Adam drive to Sunset and leave Adam's car there.
  6. They drive back to Steelhead in Eric's car with Adam's key — in a bag where it won't get wet.
On the water
  1. Get in the river.
  2. Float for four to five hours.
Getting home
  1. Non-drivers start deflating at Sunset.
  2. Eric and Adam drive Adam's car back to Steelhead.
  3. They drive both cars back to Sunset.
  4. Adam, Arden, Andreina and Corinne head back to Oakland and Los Gatos.
  5. Laura, Eric, Allison and Julia head back to SF.
